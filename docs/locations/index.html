Oshorial

This campaign takes place in the world called Oshorial. It consists of 2 main continents:

  • Osoth: East
  • Ebix: West

There are 5 major Empires or Countries.

  • Drojan Empire
  • Craz Glad
  • Prozar Empire
  • Kingdom of Dirons
  • Vaba Dynasty
