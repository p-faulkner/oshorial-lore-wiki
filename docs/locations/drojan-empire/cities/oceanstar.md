# Oceanstar
**Port City · Drojan Empire · Continent of Osoth**

**Ruler:** Cassius au Telemachus
**Status:** Unofficial capital of the empire

---

## Overview

Oceanstar is not the seat of the Drojan Empire. It will tell you this freely, and then it will take your money, pour you a drink, show you something you've never seen before, and leave you wondering why the actual capital even matters. It is the empire's largest port city and its true beating heart — the place where every trade route eventually leads, where every idea eventually lands, and where every vice is available for the right price.

The city sprawls along the coastline in controlled chaos: grand university towers rising behind fish markets, amphitheaters wedged between warehouse districts, temples to a dozen gods sharing streets with gambling dens. The harbor never sleeps. The city barely does either.

> *"If you can't find it in Oceanstar, it doesn't exist. And if it doesn't exist, someone here will sell you a convincing imitation."*
> — Common traveler's saying

---

## The City

Oceanstar is everything the empire claims to be, compressed into a single place. Commerce, scholarship, artistry, and depravity exist here not in spite of each other but because of each other. The universities draw the scholars; the scholars draw the booksellers, the debate halls, the coffee houses. The port draws the merchants; the merchants draw the entertainers, the con artists, the pleasure houses. Every layer of the city feeds the next.

The harbor district is the city's loudest, most chaotic face — a crush of dockhands, sailors, traders, and thieves where a dozen languages compete at full volume and a fortune can change hands in an alley. Deeper into the city, the streets widen, the buildings grow taller and cleaner, and the noise becomes the more refined hum of money and influence. The heights above the bay are patrician territory: walled estates, private gardens, and the kind of silence that only wealth can purchase in a city this loud.

The divides that run through the empire run through Oceanstar too, only here they are impossible to ignore — the richest and poorest residents of the empire live within a mile of each other and rarely make eye contact.

## What You Can Find Here

- **Trade** — If it is made, grown, caught, or stolen anywhere on Osoth, it passes through Oceanstar's harbor. The markets range from vast legitimate trading halls to back-alley stalls where the provenance of goods is best left unasked.
- **Knowledge** — The University of Oceanstar is one of the oldest and most prestigious institutions in the empire. Its scholars are brilliant, its politics are vicious, and its library contains things the Senate would prefer remained unread.
- **Art & Performance** — Theater, music, poetry, and spectacle of every variety. Oceanstar's stages have launched careers and ended reputations. The city's artists consider themselves a cut above those in the capital, and they are probably right.
- **Pleasure** — The city's taverns, bathhouses, gambling halls, and brothels are legendary. This is, notably, where Cassius au Telemachus spent the majority of his formative years.
- **Mischief** — Oceanstar has a thieves' quarter, a smugglers' network, a black market for magical goods, and no shortage of people willing to make a problem disappear for the right fee. The city guard is competent, underpaid, and selectively blind.

## Ruler: Cassius au Telemachus

Cassius is twenty-three years old and looks like a man who has recently been told some very bad news and has not yet finished processing it.

He was never supposed to rule. As the younger son of the previous lord governor, his role in life was to be charming at parties, spend his allowance creatively, and stay out of the way of actual governance. He was excellent at this. He knew every tavern keeper in the harbor district by name, had strong opinions about which bathhouses had the best wine, and was genuinely beloved by people whose approval does not typically impress the Senate.

Then his father died — suddenly, without warning — and the succession fell to Cassius.

He is, by every formal measure, unqualified. He has no military experience, no legal training, no deep knowledge of trade policy or civic administration. His first month in office was, by most accounts, a disaster of well-meaning incompetence. His second month was only somewhat better.

But there is something in Cassius that his detractors underestimate: he actually wants to get it right. Not out of ambition — ambition was never his vice — but out of a stubborn, almost embarrassed sense of obligation. He knew his father. He loved his father. He is not willing to let that legacy collapse on his watch.

He has begun surrounding himself with competent advisors, which required first admitting he needed them — not a small thing for a Telemachus. He listens more than he speaks in council, which the older administrators find unsettling but not unwelcome. He still slips away to the harbor district more often than is strictly appropriate for a lord governor. His advisors have stopped trying to prevent this, partly because it's impossible, and partly because he comes back with information about the city that no formal report would ever contain.

Cassius au Telemachus is not the ruler Oceanstar expected. He may yet become the one it needs.

> *"He asked me what the fish cost. Not to haggle — just to know. I've never had a lord ask me that before."*
> — Harbor market vendor, overheard

## Atmosphere & Feel

Oceanstar is loud, layered, and alive. It smells like salt water and spice and things frying in oil. It sounds like a dozen arguments happening simultaneously in different languages. It looks, depending on where you stand, like either the most magnificent city in the world or the most exhausting one — and often both at the same time.

It is the kind of place where a disgraced scholar and a wealthy merchant and a wanted criminal can share a table in a tavern, and all three will leave having gotten something they needed. It rewards the bold, fleeces the naive, and has a talent for making everyone feel, for at least a little while, that anything is possible here.

The empire flows through Oceanstar like blood through a heart. Take the city away and the whole body falters. Everyone knows this. Including, increasingly, Cassius.
