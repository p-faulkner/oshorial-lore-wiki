Ukim Thunderfoot - future use

  • Name: Ukim Thunderfoot
  • Role: Guildmaster, Deepacre Adventurers Guild
  • Faction / Allegiance: Deepacre Adventurers Guild (founded by him); secret member of an ancient and dying Paladin order that serves an entity older than the gods
  • First Appearance: Scene 1 — the Deepacre Adventurers Guild hall
  • Public Face: A warm, plain-speaking retired dwarf who is grateful for the company and apologetic about the modesty of the work. Handshake first, questions second.
  • Private Goal: He wants to build something that outlasts him — a guild that gives Deepacre a layer of protection and purpose it doesn't know it needs yet. He chose this town. He wants to have chosen right.
  • Secret: He's closer to the end of his years than he lets on. The body that ended his career is still declining. He started the guild now because he felt he was running out of time to start it at all. More than the guild, he carries the weight of a dying order — an ancient brotherhood of Paladins who serve an entity older than the gods themselves. He may be one of the last. The guild is genuine, but it is also a quiet search. He is watching the party from the moment they walk through the door.
  • Voice / Mannerisms: Speaks in short, direct sentences. Doesn't complain. Fills silences with offers — a drink, a seat, an apology for the state of the place. Calls everyone "friend" without it feeling hollow. Laughs quietly at his own expense.
  • Stat Block Reference: Veteran (MM p. 350) — refluffed as a dwarf paladin. No longer combat-ready but retains the bearing of one who was.
  • Adventure Links: Scene 1 (introduction, issues the job); Scene 6 (party debrief, reacts to the summoning evidence, takes findings to the mayor)
