    Rock Hammerfirst - The Broken Axe of Richmondtree

    • Level: 1
    • Species: Dwarf
    • Class: Fighter

    Backstory

    • Home City: Richmondtree
    • Home Empire: Drojan Empire

    Roleplay Hooks

    • Rock distrusts religion—especially Moradin’s followers.
    • He hates confinement, darkness, and chains.
    • Loud forge noises or the smell of burning metal may trigger memories.
    • He struggles with survivor’s guilt and desertion.
    • Secretly, he wonders if his clan—or any survivors—still live.
    • He may one day face the northern extremists again… and decide what kind of dwarf he truly is.

    Story

    Rock Hammerfist was born beneath the granite peaks of the Drojan Empire, in the modest but proud mining town of Richmondtree. Like most dwarves, his life was shaped early by stone, steel, and tradition. His clan were miners and smiths—not nobles, not legends—but sturdy folk who believed a well-made tool was as honorable as a well-fought battle.

Rock stood out even among dwarves. Broad-shouldered, stubborn, and fiercely loyal, he favored both the crushing certainty of a battle axe and the distant precision of a longbow—an unusual pairing that earned him some teasing in his youth. Still, no one doubted his resolve.

The Sundering of the Mountains

When Rock came of age, the Drojan Empire fractured.

The northern clans, entrenched deep within the sacred peaks, fell under the influence of radical priests devoted to Moradin the Soulforger. But this was not the Moradin of balance and creation. Their doctrine twisted his teachings into something harsh and unforgiving:

They believed Moradin demanded purity through suffering—that only dwarves who endured pain, fire, and brutal trials were worthy of existence. Weakness was heresy. Mercy was betrayal. And all who refused this doctrine were considered flawed metal to be reforged… or destroyed.

The southern clans rejected this extremism, forming the Southern Resistance—fighters, refugees, and traditionalists who believed Moradin valued craftsmanship, community, and perseverance, not cruelty.

The mountains themselves became the dividing line. Brother fought brother. Clan turned against clan.

War and Capture

Rock joined the Southern Resistance, not out of glory—but out of duty. Richmondtree lay too close to the dividing range. If the north advanced, his home would fall.

But Rock never became a hero of the battlefield.

His unit saw only scattered skirmishes before they were ambushed in a narrow pass. Outnumbered and cut off, Rock fought hard—but not hard enough. He was captured.

The Years in the Dark

The northern extremists did not kill their prisoners.

They reforged them.

Rock endured years of torture in the deep forges beneath the mountains. They burned him, starved him, broke his bones, and forced him through cruel “trials” meant to purge weakness. All the while, they preached their twisted faith—demanding he accept that suffering was divine.

But Rock never broke.

Not in spirit.

Eventually, they believed his body had failed him. Broken and unmoving, he was thrown into a refuse pit—a grave for those who “failed the forge.”

The Climb Back to Life

But dwarves are stubborn things.

Rock woke.

Bruised, scarred, and half-dead, he clawed his way out of the pit. No armor. No weapons. Just grit, rage, and the faint memory of who he used to be.

He fled south.

Ashes of Home

When Rock finally reached Richmondtree, he found nothing but ruin.

The town had been burned to the ground—whether by northern forces, southern retreat, or something worse, he never learned. The mines were collapsed. The forges cold. The people gone.

Everything he fought for… erased.

The Desertion

Something in Rock broke that day.

Not like the torturers had tried to break him—but something deeper.

He turned his back on the war.

No final stand. No revenge. No oath.

Just silence.

Rock Hammerfist deserted the Southern Resistance and kept walking south, away from the mountains, away from Moradin, away from everything he once believed.

Green Flower

Eventually, he arrived in the distant settlement of Green Flower—a place far removed from dwarven politics and mountain wars.

Here, Rock is something new.

Not a soldier. Not a prisoner.

Just a survivor.

But the past doesn’t stay buried forever.

The scars on his body tell stories. The rage in his heart hasn’t cooled. And somewhere deep within, the question still burns:

Was he reforged… or simply broken?
